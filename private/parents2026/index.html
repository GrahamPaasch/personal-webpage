💡 Ideas to pick from

🌊 Barton SpringsA spring-fed pool that stays about 68°F all year. Lovely to walk around even if nobody swims.
🎄 Trail of LightsAustin's big holiday light walk in Zilker Park, usually open in December evenings. Check the dates and tickets.
🚶 Lady Bird Lake trailFlat, easy walking along the river downtown, with great skyline views.
🏛️ Texas State CapitolFree tours, and it's taller than the U.S. Capitol.
📜 Bullock Texas State History MuseumBig, well done, and an easy few hours.
🇺🇸 LBJ Presidential LibraryOn the UT campus. Good for a history afternoon.
🌅 Mount BonnellA short climb to the best view of the river at sunset.
