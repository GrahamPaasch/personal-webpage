01 / field note

A practice can be both precise and alive.

For more than a decade I have worked in networks: designing the conditions under which systems keep their promises. Away from the rack, I practice the same attention with a viola, with a set of juggling clubs, and with small local machines that can tell stories, speak, and make sound without sending the raw material somewhere else.

Current curiosity: can a fairytale film studio run with enough character, craft, and restraint on one person’s own GPU? I’m finding out by building the tools as I need them.
