Pixel Opera

Real operas staged as 16-bit RPG cutscenes (spec 40). Four scenes; each sung scene is published three ways so the orchestra (A: real sampled orchestra, B: console-style sound) and the English translation (A: fresh plain modern, B: a 19th-century public-domain version) can be judged side by side. The dance has no words, so audio A/B only.

Methods bake-off (2026-11-27): the same 38 s six ways, head to head

Art round 2 (2026-11-27): stills of the redrawn Figaro scene

Art test: one scene, three ways

After the pilot verdict on the art (characters and room disconnected, generic sprites), Figaro's "Non più andrai" was re-rendered with the real orchestra and the revised translation in three art directions. Same audio, same timeline; only the pictures differ.

  • Art A: Liberated Pixel Cup all the wayRoom built from LPC castle-interior tilesets; custom LPC costumes (barber's jacket and hairnet, page's tabard on the teen body).
  • Art B: generated room and sprite sheets, matchedRoom and character pose sheets generated in one pass with the same pixel-art prompt, cut once, same pixel density, one 48-colour palette.
  • Art C: original hand-authored pixel artSprites designed pixel by pixel as palette-indexed grids (32x48, idle/walk/point/sweep/bow/flinch) and a hand-drawn room in the same 37-colour palette.
