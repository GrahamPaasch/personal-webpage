2Components

Network OS · all 5 switches

SONiC (Software for Open Networking in the Cloud)

  • Open-source Linux network OS, open-sourced by Microsoft in 2016, now under the Linux Foundation
  • Uses FRRouting for BGP, the same routing suite as the lab
  • Installs onto white-box switches through ONIE

Role in the topology: Runs on every spine and leaf. Your router bgp / BGP-unnumbered config from the lab carries over almost line for line.

Free open source; paid Enterprise SONiC bundles also exist
OCP: SONiC →SONiC Foundation →
