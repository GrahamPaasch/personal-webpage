Trigger-Action Plans (TAPs)

Build reliable if-then behavior loops: tiny action, concrete cue, repeated rehearsal.

1. Identify the behavior

What do you want to do more reliably?

2. Design the trigger

Make it sensory-specific. What will you see, hear, or feel right before you want to act?

Good trigger: "When I close my laptop after lunch."
Good trigger: "When I feel my phone buzz with my 8:00 PM reminder."
Bad trigger: "When I have time."
Bad trigger: "When I feel motivated."
3. Design the action

Make it tiny and immediate (about 5 seconds). What is the smallest physical action that starts the behavior?

4. TAP preview
