Freedom of Action

Find where your options are narrower than they need to be, separate real limits from assumed ones, and choose your next move on purpose.

1. Describe the situation where you feel stuck
Name the concrete context, what decision is in front of you, and what "stuck" feels like right now.
2. Identify the default frame / assumed path
What story is currently running in your head about "how this has to go"?
3. List constraints (tag as real / assumed / emotional)
Break out every factor limiting your choices. Tag each one so you can stop treating all constraints as equally fixed.
4. Generate alternative options
List options beyond the default path. Include small moves, hybrids, and reversible experiments.
5. Detect overbidding with a slider
How much are you demanding an idealized outcome instead of a workable next step?
5
6. Identify the real need
Under your strategy and preferences, what core need are you actually trying to protect or satisfy?
7. Make a fair bid
Ask for what the real need requires, without over-claiming or demanding impossible guarantees.
8. Choose an action and explain why
Pick one concrete action now, and name the values it serves so your decision is legible to yourself.
9. Design a next experiment
Make the next step testable: what will you do, by when, and what evidence will count as signal?
