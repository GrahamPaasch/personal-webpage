Aversion Factoring

Break down what you're avoiding into concrete sub-aversions, then solve each one.

1. Name the aversion
What are you avoiding or procrastinating on? Be specific.
2. Decompose into sub-aversions
What specifically feels bad about this? List each distinct thing that makes you flinch. Aim for 3-7.
3. Button test each sub-aversion
For each sub-aversion: if you could press a magic button and this specific thing wasn't a problem, would you still avoid the task?
4. Generate solutions for blockers
For each sub-aversion you marked as a blocker, brainstorm a concrete fix. Classify each solution.
5. Remaining resistance check
Imagine doing the thing now, with all your solutions in place. How much aversion remains?
