Goal Factoring

Decompose what you are doing into the goals it serves, then design a better strategy that gets you what you actually want.

1. Name the action or habit to examine
Pick one concrete pattern, not your whole life. This works best when the action is specific and current.
2. Extract underlying goals
List what this action is trying to do for you. Tag each goal as pursue, avoid, or identity.
3. Rate how well the current action serves each goal
Use 0 to mean it does not help at all, and 10 to mean it serves the goal extremely well.
4. Brainstorm alternative strategies
Generate options that serve the same goals with fewer downsides. Quantity first, quality second.
5. Design your new plan
Pick one strategy or hybrid. Define exactly what you will do, when, and how you will handle friction.
6. Replacement test
Imagine your new plan is available right now. Which statement feels most true?
