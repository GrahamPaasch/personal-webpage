Double Crux

Find the belief that actually changes each side's mind, then test that belief directly.

Step 1 - Define the disagreement

A useful Double Crux starts with one concrete disagreement and a good steelman of both positions.

70% confident
65% confident
Target mindset: curiosity over winning. You are looking for "what would make me change my mind," not "how do I beat them."

Step 2 - Your crux

Your crux is the key belief that props up your position. If this belief falls, your view should shift.

Step 3 - Their crux (best guess)

Do your best to model what would really change the other side's mind. Ask directly if possible.

If you cannot explain the other side well enough that they say "yes, that is my view," you are not at crux yet.

Step 4 - Shared tests

List observations that would update both sides. Good tests are concrete, time-bounded, and measurable.

Step 5 - Double Crux summary

Use this summary to run the actual discussion. The goal is mutual update, not rhetorical victory.
