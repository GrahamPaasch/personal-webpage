How to play

Guide the falling shapes into complete rows. Every row you complete dissolves into light that flies into the world and becomes part of it — fish in the deep, fireflies in the forest, stars over the dunes.

You are playing the music. Each move, turn and drop is a note in the stage's key, landing on the beat. Clear more lines at once for bigger flourishes; chain clears for combos that climb the scale. The better you play, the fuller the band.

← → move   ↓ soft drop   Space hard drop   ↑/X rotate ↻   Z/Ctrl rotate ↺   A 180°   C/Shift hold   Esc/P pause

Gamepad: D-pad moves, A/B rotate, X or LB hold, Y 180°, RB or up hard drop, Start pause.

Journey: six worlds, 104 lines. Endless: the worlds cycle forever and the speed keeps rising.
