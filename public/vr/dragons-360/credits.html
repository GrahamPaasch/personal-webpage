Credits: Two Dragons, October (360 film)

Everything seen is a real, licensed asset, rendered in Blender 4.2 (Cycles) on this machine.

The dragons

  • "Scales", the adult dragon from Sintel (rigged model, 4K painted textures, sculpted scales, flying cycle ad_flying-normal). (C) Blender Foundation, www.sintel.org. Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0). Source: the Sintel DATA disc (pro/chars/dragon_adult*.blend, textures/), mirrored at https://archive.org/details/sintel-dvd. Changes made here: the sculpted multires detail baked into the mesh (in Blender 2.79), materials rebuilt for Cycles, each of the two dragons tinted (Ember red, Tarn teal), a decimated copy for distant shots, new flight paths and glide/flap timing.

The world (Poly Haven, CC0, https://polyhaven.com)

  • Sky and light: HDRI "The Sky is On Fire" (the_sky_is_on_fire, 8K), by Poly Haven. The city horizon in it is remapped away.
  • Scanned rocks: boulder_01, rock_07, rock_09, rock_face_01, rock_face_02.
  • Trees: island_tree_01, island_tree_02, island_tree_03, tree_small_02 (recoloured to autumn), pine_tree_01, fir_tree_01.
  • Ground and rock textures: lichen_rock, dry_decay_leaves, forest_leaves_04, rock_face_03, aerial_grass_rock.

Leaves (ambientCG, CC0, https://ambientcg.com)

  • Leaf scans LeafSet007, LeafSet008, LeafSet012, LeafSet021, LeafSet027, LeafSet028, cut into 53 single leaves, for the falling leaves and the leaves on the ledge.

The moon (NASA, public domain)

  • CGI Moon Kit, NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720: LRO/LROC colour map (lroc_color_poles_4k.tif) and LOLA elevation (ldem_16_uint.tif). Ernie Wright (USRA), Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).

Sound

  • Built from the sounds of the interactive version, Two Dragons, October (https://www.grahampaasch.com/vr/dragons), which are generated by its own code (wind, birds, wingbeats, the rush of air, roars), placed here by this film's flight.

Software

  • Blender 4.2 (GPL), Cycles with OptiX. three.js r160 (MIT) for the player. ffmpeg (x265, x264) for the encodes.
