Two Dragons, October

A rock ledge over a Wisconsin valley as the sun goes down. Two dragons come in out of the light, low over the lake, and half a minute in, right over your head.

A 75-second photoreal 360° film in stereo 3D. Put on headphones, press Enter VR, and look all around: behind you, up, and down over the edge.
