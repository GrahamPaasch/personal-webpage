♞ Wraparound Chess


Changed rules

  • The board is a cylinder. The a-file and h-file touch. Anything sliding or jumping sideways off one edge comes back on the other. The faded columns show the squares across the seam.
  • No check, no checkmate. You win by actually capturing the king. Leaving your king hanging is legal, and fatal.
  • Pawns capture across the seam too, and promote to a queen on the last rank. No castling, no en passant.
