2. Cold-weather clothing

Sizes: XL tops, 40x30 pants. The everyday preference is cotton, but the research is clear on one point: cotton is the worst fabric against the skin in cold, wet weather. It soaks up sweat and rain, holds it, and pulls heat out of the body once it's damp. Merino wool stays warm when damp, wicks moisture, dries faster, and doesn't smell after days of wear. So the plan keeps cotton where it does well, as the outer shirts and sweater, and puts wool or fleece where warmth matters.

Warm layer (top)

Best overall: Minus33 Chocorua Midweight Crew (style 705), 100% merino, XL best pick
About $80–90. 235 g/m² merino, which is the weight guides recommend for general cold-weather use. Machine washable, tagless, and the brand says it doesn't itch. Wear it under any of the cotton shirts or the sweater in the cart below: indoors it disappears, outdoors it does the heavy lifting. One shirt can be worn several days in a row without smelling, which also saves carry-on space.
Minus33 Chocorua on Amazon

Premium alternative: Smartwool Classic Thermal Merino Base Layer Crew alternative
About $115 list, often on sale. A well-reviewed 250-weight merino crew with a slimmer fit. It does the same job as the Minus33 for more money.
Smartwool Classic Thermal on Amazon (choose XL)

Budget mid-layer: Columbia Steens Mountain 2.0 Full Zip Fleece budget
About $40–65. Polyester fleece, Amazon's #1 best seller in men's fleece jackets. Zip it on over a cotton shirt for real warmth, and it stays warm when damp. For cold rain it still needs a waterproof shell on top.
Columbia Steens Mountain on Amazon

Lined pants

Best overall: Wrangler Authentics Fleece Lined Five Pocket Jean, 40x30 best pick
About $30–50, depending on size and color. Cotton denim on the outside with a polyester fleece lining. Rated 4.6 stars from nearly 10,000 reviews. Reviewers say the double layer blocks wind and keeps their legs warm without long underwear. The fleece lining stays warm even if the jeans get wet, unlike an all-cotton flannel lining. Sizing runs a little generous.
Wrangler fleece-lined jean on Amazon

All-cotton alternative: Carhartt Relaxed Fit Heavyweight Flannel-Lined 5-Pocket Jean all cotton
About $55–65. 15 oz cotton denim with a 100% cotton flannel lining, so it fits the cotton preference. It's heavy, durable, and very warm when dry, but slower to dry than the fleece-lined pair if it gets soaked. Reviewers call the seat and legs roomy.
Carhartt flannel-lined jean on Amazon

Most flexible: Minus33 Kancamagus Midweight merino bottoms, under the IZOD chinos layering
About $80. The same 235 g/m² merino as the top pick, rated for about 30°F down to 0°F while active. Lined jeans can get too hot in heated museums, trains, and restaurants. A thin wool layer under the chinos already in the cart is warm outdoors, comfortable indoors, and packs small.
Minus33 Kancamagus on Amazon (choose XL)
