  • ●Packets ride arcs and land on the beat line. Every packet that lands is instantly re-thrown — you choose its TTL: how many beats until it lands again.
  • ⌨Press 1–9, or tap a slot / number button, to set the TTL of the pulsing packet. You can queue throws ahead of the beat; ⌫ undoes.
  • ✕Two packets can never share a beat. Occupied slots are closed to you.
  • ◆Land on gold beats: bonus and +1 multiplier. Let one pass empty and the multiplier slips.
  • ✺Never land in a black hole, and never let a packet land with no TTL set — each costs a link. Three links.
  • ∑A throw scores TTL². The average TTL always equals the packet count, so variance is points: 5‑3‑1 beats 3‑3‑3. Loop a pattern and it's recognised as a siteswap for combo bonuses.
